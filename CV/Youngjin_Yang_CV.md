## Selected Research Projects

**circRNA Translation Optimization** — Chung-Ang University · 2026  
Used a Firefly/Gaussia dual-luciferase reporter system to compare IRES candidates, with FHB as the WT normalization reference. Follow-up work examined selected IRES and UTR combinations, repeatability, and sequence/structure interactions.

**SZ1 IRES Mutation Landscape** — Independent Project · 2026  
Generated and analyzed 1,950 single-nucleotide substitutions across the 650-nt SZ1 IRES. Used Python and RNAfold to calculate MFE, ΔMFE, pairing-state changes, and local structure differences, then connected structural features to nine experimental labels for exploratory analysis. Built a GitHub-hosted interactive landscape to inspect variants and their computed features. With only nine experimental labels, the machine-learning analysis remains exploratory and is not a validated prediction model.

**IRES Screening & Reproducibility Analysis** — 2026  
Screened approximately 100 candidate conditions in HEK293T cells. Technical duplicates were measured after 24 hours and normalized to WT (FHB) using Gaussia/Firefly ratios. Repeated screening was used to assess reproducibility and select candidates for follow-up optimization.
