Youngjin Yang.

About Me

My path in life sciences has taken me across different areas of biological research. In 2023, I worked in a regenerative medicine research laboratory at Dongguk University, where I gained experience with cell-based research, FACS, and in vivo experiments.

After completing my military service as an Air Force Military Police, I returned to academia and joined a research laboratory at Chung-Ang University in 2026, where I worked on RNA engineering and circRNA translation optimization.

Through these experiences, I have become interested in experimental optimization, biological systems engineering, and quantitative evaluation. Rather than focusing on a single biological technique, I want to understand how biological systems behave under different conditions and how experimental results can be translated into more reliable and reproducible processes.
