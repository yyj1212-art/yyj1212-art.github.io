RESEARCH EXPERIENCE · 2023

Dongguk University.

Undergraduate research experience in cell-based regenerative medicine at Im Gun-il Lab.

Research Context

I participated in research related to adipose-derived stem cells (ADSCs), spheroid culture, and cartilage regeneration. My role was primarily hands-on experimental support across cell and molecular biology workflows and in vivo experiments.

Experimental Experience

CELL-BASED WORK
ADSC & spheroid culture

Worked with adipose-derived and bone marrow-derived cell samples obtained through Dongguk University Hospital. Participated in cell culture, spheroid culture, and bone marrow preparation workflows.

MOLECULAR ANALYSIS
PCR & Western blot

Participated in PCR / RT-qPCR and Western blot experiments used to evaluate cellular and molecular changes in the research workflow.

TISSUE PROCESSING
Microtome experience

Gained hands-on experience with tissue sectioning using a microtome as part of downstream tissue analysis.

FACS Experience

I was introduced to FACS-based cellular analysis by observing and learning the workflow alongside a researcher. I did not independently perform the full analysis or interpret the final datasets, so I present this as an exposure to the experimental workflow rather than as an independent FACS analysis skill.

In Vivo Experience

  • Mouse and rabbit handling and experimental preparation
  • Anesthesia injection and preparation procedures
  • Hair removal and animal handling
  • Direct intra-articular administration
  • Weekly GFP imaging to observe persistence over time
  • Organization and recording of experimental observations

What I Learned

This experience gave me an early foundation in cell-based research and exposed me to the connection between in vitro experiments and in vivo evaluation. It also helped me become comfortable moving between cell culture, molecular assays, tissue processing, and animal experiments.
